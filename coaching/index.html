What a session is

A coach walks through the material with you on the call. The session is yours. We do not write the paper, take the exam, or submit the work.

Subject tutoring

Math, statistics, accounting, business, and other college subjects. We work the problem with you until the method is clear.

Study coaching

A plan for the week: what to review, what to practice, and what to do first before a deadline.

Draft feedback

Comments on a paper you wrote: structure, citations, and clarity. You keep your own wording.
