Software / digital

Learning portal subscription

This is the main product. After payment you receive a portal login by email, usually within one business day.

Human services

Live tutoring tied to the portal

Optional sessions with a tutor, sold with portal access. Tutoring supports the same subjects you practice in the software.

  • A scheduled 45-minute session on the course you name
  • A walkthrough of the concept or problem you are stuck on

  • Extra sessions are $55 each

What we do not sell. HomeworkGuy does not write papers, take exams, complete graded assignments, or log into a student’s school account. The AI coach explains steps and builds practice. You submit your own work.
