Refund Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This policy covers purchases of the HomeworkGuy learning portal and related tutoring. It is part of our Terms. Payments and refunds are processed by Paddle.com, the merchant of record. We do not refund buyers directly to a card.

1. How to request a refund

Use either of these:

  • Email support@homeworkguy.live from the address on the purchase. Include the plan name and the date you paid.
  • Open the receipt or “Manage subscription” link in your Paddle confirmation email and request a refund there.

We reply within two business days. If the request qualifies, we ask Paddle to refund the original payment method. Paddle aims to send an approved refund within 14 days of approval. Your bank may take longer to show it.

A refund ends portal access for the charge that was refunded.

2. First subscription charge — 14 days

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment for Portal ($29/month), Portal + AI Coach ($49/month), or Portal + AI Coach + Tutoring ($129/month) if your use has stayed light. Light use means all of the following are true:

  • you have finished fewer than five practice quizzes
  • you have not saved more than one AI practice set
  • you have not attended a tutoring session
  • we have not already sent draft feedback

If you have used the product past that point, we may decline the refund or refund only the unused portion of a tutoring plan. Asking within 14 days does not by itself guarantee a refund once the portal or a session has been used as described above.

If we never send a working login within two business days of payment, you can request a full refund of that charge even if the 14 days have not started cleanly, and we will approve it.

3. Renewal charges — 14 days

Monthly plans renew until you cancel. You can request a full refund of a renewal within 14 days of that renewal charge if you have not used the portal and have not taken a tutoring session during the new period. Cancel as well, or the next month will renew again.

To avoid a renewal, cancel before the renewal date from the Paddle receipt link or by emailing support@homeworkguy.live. Instructions are also on the pricing section.

4. Tutoring sessions — $55 and sessions inside the $129 plan

  • Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the session for a full refund of a $55 session, or to keep the session credit on the $129 plan.
  • A session you attend, or miss without 24 hours’ notice, is not refunded.
  • Draft feedback that we have already delivered is not refunded.
  • If we cancel and do not reschedule at a time you accept, that session is refunded in full. On the $129 plan we either restore the credit or refund a proportionate share of $55 for that session.

5. What is not refunded

  • Charges older than 14 days, except the login-failure case in section 2 and a session we cancelled
  • Periods you already used, including finished quizzes past the light-use limit, AI practice sets you saved, and completed tutoring
  • Accounts closed for misuse under the Terms

This policy does not limit rights you have under consumer law that cannot be waived. Customers in the EU and UK may have a 14-day withdrawal right for digital services. If you asked us to start access immediately, you acknowledge that access begins during that period and that a refund may be reduced to reflect use, as the law allows.
